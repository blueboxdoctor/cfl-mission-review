Quiet, steady obedience

Quiet FaithfulnessVideo goes here — waiting on the final video link

Dear Friends,

In a restless, noisy age, God calls His people to something better — quiet, steady obedience (1 Thess. 4:11).

Not driven by:

  • Market trends
  • Social media posts
  • Or momentary applause . . .

But by faithful devotion in the home, the church, and daily work.

This is the heartbeat of Church and Family Life’s mission: To Proclaim Quiet Faithfulness through the Sufficiency of Scripture.

Every book we publish, podcast we produce, and conference we host is fueled by this aim. We want to see:

  • Fathers daily reading the Scriptures to their children
  • Families joyfully singing together
  • Christians gathering faithfully with the saints
  • Husbands loving their wives
  • Mothers pouring themselves into their homes
  • Church leaders and members serving without seeking acclaim

Christians quietly doing the work God has put before them — day by day, year after year.

2026 was a remarkable year for advancing this mission. We invite you to review these recent milestones — along with our goals for 2027 — as you plan your year-end giving.

Thank you for standing with us as we encourage churches and families to simply be faithful — and to trust God, over time, with the increase (Gal. 6:9).

Your brother in Christ,

  • A mother does not build a faithful home through a handful of memorable moments.

Our message is this: Don’t chase “viral.” Build what lasts through ordinary faithfulness.
